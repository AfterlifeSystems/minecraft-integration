  Step 1: Install a Mod LoaderDownload the installer for Forge or Fabric that matches your exact Minecraft game version (e.g., 1.21).Run the installer and select Install Client.Open the official Minecraft Launcher, choose the new Forge or Fabric profile from the bottom-left dropdown, and launch the game once to create the necessary files. Then close the game. 
  
  Step 2: Download Your ModsGo to a trusted site like Modrinth or CurseForge.Search for mods that match your exact Minecraft version and your chosen mod loader (Forge or Fabric).Download the .jar mod files. Note that Fabric often requires the Fabric API mod to work.
  
  Step 3: Put Mods in the FolderOpen your computer's run or search tool.Windows users type %appdata% and open .minecraft. Mac users go to ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft.Find or create a folder named mods.Move your downloaded .jar files into the mods folder.
  
  Step 4: Play the GameOpen the Minecraft Launcher.Select your Forge or Fabric profile.Click Play. Your mods will load automatically. \
